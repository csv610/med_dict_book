The NHS inform A–Z is the source list for this audit. It contains 395 unique headings in the reviewed index: **329** are represented in the dictionary and **66** are not represented as distinct terms.

## Matching policy

- Each item below comes from the NHS inform A–Z page, not from the dictionary.
- Dictionary membership was checked against `\medterm{...}` headwords and synonym lines extracted directly from `chapters/med_terms_*.tex`.
- “Present” ignores capitalization, curly/straight apostrophes, repeated whitespace, standard UK/US spelling variants, and explicit dictionary synonyms or alternate terms.
- “Absent” means no exact, synonym, base-term, or clearly documented equivalent was found in the chapter source. Related entries are identified as related, not exact matches.
