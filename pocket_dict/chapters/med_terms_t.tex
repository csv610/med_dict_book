\medterm{Trigger Finger Release} A common minor hand surgery procedure performed to cure trigger finger or trigger thumb (stenosing tenosynovitis) when rest, splinting, or corticosteroid injections fail to relieve symptoms. Releasing this tight fibrous tunnel widens the flexor sheath, allowing the swollen or nodular tendon to glide smoothly through without catching, popping, clicking, or painfully locking into the palm. Performed either through a traditional open technique or percutaneously with a needle, trigger finger release provides permanent relief, immediate finger movement, and a rapid return to normal daily hand use.
