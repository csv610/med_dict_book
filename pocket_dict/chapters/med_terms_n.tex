\medterm{Neuroendocrine tumors} Tumors arising from cells that receive signals from the nervous system and produce hormones; they may be benign or malignant.
\textbf{Synonyms}:

\medterm{NF2-related schwannomatosis (neurofibromatosis type 2)} A genetic disorder characterized by tumors involving nerves, especially bilateral vestibular schwannomas, with possible hearing loss, balance problems, and other nerve tumors.
