\medterm{Knee} The joint between the thighbone, shinbone, and kneecap. It bends and straightens the leg and is supported by ligaments, cartilage, muscles, and tendons.

\medterm{Knee Disorders} Problems affecting the knee joint, including injuries to ligaments or menisci, arthritis, bursitis, tendon disorders, infection, and fractures. They may cause pain, swelling, stiffness, instability, locking, or difficulty bearing weight.

\medterm{Knee Effusion} An abnormal buildup of fluid inside the knee joint. It can cause swelling, stiffness, and limited movement and may result from injury, arthritis, bleeding, or infection.
