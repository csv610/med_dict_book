\medterm{Ziehl-Neelsen Stain} A specialized bacteriological differential staining technique (acid-fast stain) developed to visualize and identify acid-fast bacilli (AFB), predominantly members of the genus \textit{Mycobacterium} (including \textit{Mycobacterium tuberculosis}, \textit{M. leprae}, and nontuberculous mycobacteria) and related actinomycetes (\textit{Nocardia}, \textit{Cryptosporidium}, and \textit{Isospora}).
