
\medterm{Z-Plasty}-- Surgical technique for contracture release using Z-shaped incision. Lengthens tissue,
changes scar orientation.

\medterm{Zalcitabine}-- A nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (NRTI) that was previously used in
combination antiretroviral therapy for HIV-1 infection but is now rarely used because
more effective and better-tolerated agents are available.

\medterm{Zavanelli Maneuver}-- The final, rarely performed maneuver for an irreducible shoulder dystocia: the fetal
head is replaced into the vagina (cephalic replacement) and the patient undergoes
emergency cesarean delivery.

\medterm{Zellweger Syndrome}-- The severe end of the Zellweger spectrum of peroxisomal biogenesis disorders, usually
caused by PEX-gene variants. It presents in neonates with hypotonia, seizures, feeding
difficulty, distinctive facial features, liver dysfunction, neuronal migration defects,
and profound developmental impairment.

\medterm{Zenker Diverticulum}-- Pulsion diverticulum at the junction of the thyropharyngeus and cricopharyngeus muscles
in the proximal esophagus. It presents with dysphagia, regurgitation of undigested food,
halitosis, and aspiration. Diagnosis is by barium swallow showing the characteristic
outpouching.

\medterm{Zenker's diverticulum}-- A pouch formed through the wall near the upper esophagus, causing difficulty swallowing,
regurgitation, cough, or aspiration.

\medterm{Zidovudine (AZT)}-- A thymidine-analogue nucleoside reverse-transcriptase inhibitor that is phosphorylated
intracellularly and incorporated into HIV DNA, causing chain termination. It is now used
selectively because combination antiretroviral regimens have better tolerability and
resistance profiles.

\medterm{Ziehl-Neelsen Stain}-- An acid-fast stain retaining carbol fuchsin in waxy mycolic-acid cell walls despite
acid-alcohol decolorization, with methylene blue counterstain rendering acid-fast
organisms red. Clinically important for Mycobacterium tuberculosis, M. leprae, Nocardia,
and Cryptosporidium oocysts, it is a rapid initial sputum test for tuberculosis.

\medterm{Zieve's Syndrome}-- A triad of haemolytic anemia, hyperlipidaemia, and jaundice occurring in the setting of
chronic alcohol use disorder and alcoholic liver disease (acute alcoholic
steatohepatitis or alcoholic cirrhosis).

\medterm{Zika}-- Zika virus: a mosquito-borne (Aedes) flavivirus, also sexually transmitted. Usually
asymptomatic or mild (fever, rash, conjunctivitis, arthralgia). Major concern:
congenital Zika syndrome if acquired in pregnancy—microcephaly, brain abnormalities, eye
defects; also Guillain-Barré syndrome.

\medterm{Zika virus}-- A positive-sense RNA virus belonging to the Flaviviridae family that causes mild febrile
illness. Zika virus is transmitted by Aedes mosquitoes and can also be transmitted
sexually. Infection during pregnancy can cause congenital Zika syndrome including
microcephaly. Most infections are asymptomatic or mild.

\medterm{Zinc}-- An essential trace element that acts as a structural or catalytic component of numerous
proteins and enzymes, including DNA-binding zinc-finger proteins. Deficiency may impair
growth, wound healing, immunity, taste, and reproductive function.

\medterm{Zinc Deficiency}-- A nutritional or malabsorptive disorder causing impaired wound healing, dermatitis,
alopecia, diarrhoea, taste disturbance, and increased infection risk. Causes include
inadequate intake, malabsorption, chronic diarrhoea, excessive losses, and inherited
zinc-transport disorders.

\medterm{Zinc Ointment (Zinc Oxide)}-- A topical preparation that forms a protective barrier on the skin. It is used for
irritation, moisture-associated dermatitis, minor skin injury, and selected dermatologic conditions.

\medterm{Zirconia}-- Strong ceramic material for dental restorations. High flexural strength. Can be
monolithic or layered with porcelain.

\medterm{Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome}-- A disorder caused by a gastrin-secreting tumor, or gastrinoma, leading to excessive
gastric-acid production. It may cause recurrent or difficult-to-treat peptic ulcers,
abdominal pain, diarrhea, and reflux; tumors commonly arise in the duodenum or pancreas.

\medterm{Zooglea}-- A gelatinous mass of bacteria embedded in a polysaccharide matrix.

\medterm{Zoon Balanitis}-- A chronic benign inflammatory disorder of the glans penis, usually presenting as a
sharply demarcated shiny orange-red or rust-colored patch in uncircumcised men.

\medterm{Zoonoses}-- Infectious diseases naturally transmitted between vertebrate animals and humans.
Transmission may occur through direct contact, animal products, food, water, or
arthropod vectors. Examples include rabies, brucellosis, leptospirosis, Lyme disease,
psittacosis, and toxoplasmosis.

\medterm{Zoonosis}-- Disease transmitted from animals to humans. Examples: rabies, Lyme disease, avian
influenza, COVID-19.

\medterm{Zoonotic infections}-- Infections that are naturally transmitted between animals and humans. Major zoonotic
infections include rabies, brucellosis, leptospirosis, and psittacosis. The animal
reservoir can be domestic or wild animals. Transmission occurs through direct contact,
ingestion, or vectors.

\medterm{Zoster}-- A painful, vesicular rash caused by reactivation of latent varicella-zoster virus (VZV,
human herpesvirus 3) in the dorsal root ganglia. See herpes zoster (shingles).

\medterm{Zygoma (Zygomatic Bone)}-- The cheekbone, a paired, quadrangular bone of the facial skeleton (viscerocranium) that
forms the prominence of the cheek and the lateral wall and floor of the orbit.

\medterm{Zygomatic Bone}-- A paired facial bone forming the prominence of the cheek and part of the lateral wall
and floor of the orbit. It articulates with the frontal, sphenoid, temporal, and
maxillary bones and transmits forces from the face to the cranial skeleton.

\medterm{Zygomycosis}-- An infection caused by fungi historically classified in the class Zygomycetes, including
organisms now placed among mucormycetes. It can produce rapidly invasive disease of the
nose, sinuses, orbit, lungs, skin, or gastrointestinal tract, especially in
immunocompromised patients.

\medterm{Zygote}-- The cell formed when a sperm and an oocyte fuse at fertilization. It is usually diploid and begins
the developmental process that follows fertilization.

\medterm{Zymogen}-- An inactive enzyme precursor that is converted into an active enzyme, often by cleavage.

\medterm{Zymogen (Proenzyme)}-- An inactive precursor of an enzyme that requires proteolytic cleavage to become
catalytically active.
\medterm{Zonule}-- Fibers that suspend the lens of the eye from the ciliary body and help change its shape for focusing.
