This is a selected bibliography of standard medical dictionaries and authoritative
biomedical terminology resources used to guide terminology, spelling, synonyms,
and general definitions in this dictionary. The source material is not mapped
term-by-term, so this list should not be read as a claim that every entry was
copied from every source. Clinical decisions should be based on current
guidelines, specialist references, and primary literature.
