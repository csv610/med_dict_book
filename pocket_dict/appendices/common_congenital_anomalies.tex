% Common congenital anomalies (structural abnormalities present at birth).
\small
\noindent\textit{Scope}: A congenital anomaly is a structural abnormality present at birth, whether genetic, chromosomal, or environmental in origin. This list complements the appendices of Common Diseases, Common Disorders, and Common Syndromes.

\medskip
\begin{description}
\item[Orofacial \& Head Anomalies] Cleft Lip; Cleft Palate.

\item[Neural Tube \& Brain Anomalies] Spina Bifida; Anencephaly; Encephalocele; Hydrocephalus.

\item[Thorax \& Abdominal Wall Anomalies] Congenital Diaphragmatic Hernia; Omphalocele; Gastroschisis.

\item[Digestive System Anomalies] Imperforate Anus; Hirschsprung Disease.

\item[Urinary \& Genital Anomalies] Hypospadias; Epispadias.

\item[Limb \& Skeletal Anomalies] Clubfoot; Developmental Dysplasia of the Hip; Polydactyly; Syndactyly.

\item[Eye Anomalies] Congenital Cataract.

\item[Heart \& Great-Vessel Anomalies] Congenital Heart Defects; Ventricular Septal Defect; Atrial Septal Defect; Patent Ductus Arteriosus; Tetralogy of Fallot; Coarctation of the Aorta.
\end{description}
