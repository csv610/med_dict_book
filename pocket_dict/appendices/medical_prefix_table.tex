Amyl- & A combining form referring to starch, & amylase \\

An- & A prefix meaning without, lacking, or not, & anoxia, anesthesia, and anencephaly \\

Bi- & A prefix meaning two or twice, & bilateral and bicuspid \\

Brachy- & A combining form meaning short, & brachycephaly and brachydactyly \\

Di- & A prefix meaning two or twice, & diplopia and diplegia \\

Mes-, Meso- & Combining forms meaning middle, & mesencephalon, mesoderm, and mesothelioma \\
