\noindent\textit{Scope}: This reference-oriented list organizes clinically significant medical syndromes by organ system and medical specialty. In medical terminology, a syndrome represents a characteristic constellation of concurrent signs, symptoms, or anatomical features that consistently occur together and point toward a common pathophysiological mechanism or etiology.

\medskip
\begin{description}
\item[Cardiovascular Syndromes] Acute Coronary Syndrome; Acute Aortic Syndrome; Sick Sinus Syndrome; Wolff–Parkinson–White Syndrome; Long QT Syndrome; Short QT Syndrome; Brugada Syndrome; Takotsubo Syndrome (Stress Cardiomyopathy); Eisenmenger Syndrome; Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome (POTS); Postpericardiotomy Syndrome; Dressler Syndrome; Cardiac Syndrome X; Cardiorenal Syndrome.

\item[Respiratory Syndromes] Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS); Infant Respiratory Distress Syndrome; Obesity Hypoventilation Syndrome; Upper Airway Resistance Syndrome; Hepatopulmonary Syndrome; Acute Chest Syndrome; Kartagener Syndrome.

\item[Gastrointestinal Syndromes] Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS); Malabsorption Syndrome; Short Bowel Syndrome; Dumping Syndrome; Cyclic Vomiting Syndrome; Rumination Syndrome; Mallory–Weiss Syndrome; Zollinger–Ellison Syndrome; Boerhaave Syndrome; Afferent Loop Syndrome; Cannabinoid Hyperemesis Syndrome; Superior Mesenteric Artery Syndrome; Ogilvie Syndrome.

\item[Liver \& Biliary Syndromes] Hepatorenal Syndrome; Hepatopulmonary Syndrome; Budd–Chiari Syndrome; Gilbert Syndrome; Crigler–Najjar Syndrome; Dubin–Johnson Syndrome; Rotor Syndrome; Reye Syndrome; Mirizzi Syndrome.

\item[Kidney \& Urinary Syndromes] Nephrotic Syndrome; Nephritic Syndrome; Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HUS); Alport Syndrome; Fanconi Syndrome; Goodpasture Syndrome; Hepatorenal Syndrome; Nutcracker Syndrome.

\item[Endocrine Syndromes] Cushing Syndrome; Metabolic Syndrome; Syndrome of Inappropriate Antidiuretic Hormone Secretion (SIADH); Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS); Carcinoid Syndrome; Empty Sella Syndrome; Androgen Insensitivity Syndrome; Sheehan Syndrome; Nelson Syndrome; Polyglandular Autoimmune Syndromes (PAPS Type 1 and 2); Insulin Resistance Syndrome.

\item[Neurological Syndromes] Guillain–Barré Syndrome; Miller Fisher Syndrome; Lambert–Eaton Myasthenic Syndrome (LEMS); Restless Legs Syndrome; Locked-In Syndrome; Post-Concussion Syndrome; Cauda Equina Syndrome; Conus Medullaris Syndrome; Anterior Cord Syndrome; Central Cord Syndrome; Central Pain Syndrome; Alien Hand Syndrome; CADASIL Syndrome; Foix–Alajouanine Syndrome; Horner Syndrome; Tourette Syndrome; Lennox–Gastaut Syndrome; West Syndrome; Wernicke–Korsakoff Syndrome; Brown-Séquard Syndrome; Wallenberg Syndrome (Lateral Medullary Syndrome); Isaacs Syndrome.

\item[Pain \& Neuromuscular Syndromes] Carpal Tunnel Syndrome; Cubital Tunnel Syndrome; Complex Regional Pain Syndrome (CRPS); Myofascial Pain Syndrome; Piriformis Syndrome; Compartment Syndrome; Thoracic Outlet Syndrome; Burning Mouth Syndrome; Tarsal Tunnel Syndrome; Greater Trochanteric Pain Syndrome.

\item[Psychiatric \& Behavioral Syndromes] Serotonin Syndrome; Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome; Capgras Syndrome; Cotard Syndrome; Münchausen Syndrome (Factitious Disorder); Korsakoff Syndrome; Amotivational Syndrome; Ekbom Syndrome (Delusional Parasitosis); Ganser Syndrome.

\item[Rheumatologic \& Autoimmune Syndromes] Sjögren Syndrome; Antiphospholipid Syndrome; CREST Syndrome; Felty Syndrome; Behçet Syndrome; SAPHO Syndrome; Reactive Arthritis (formerly Reiter Syndrome); Churg–Strauss Syndrome (EGPA); Caplan Syndrome.

\item[Hematological Syndromes] Myelodysplastic Syndromes; Antiphospholipid Syndrome; Evans Syndrome; Hyperviscosity Syndrome; Tumor Lysis Syndrome; Hemophagocytic Syndrome (HLH); Acute Chest Syndrome; Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura (TTP).

\item[Immunological Syndromes] Severe Combined Immunodeficiency Syndrome; Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome (AIDS); Alpha-Gal Syndrome; Antisynthetase Syndrome; Capillary Leak Syndrome; Hyper-IgE Syndrome (Job Syndrome); Autoimmune Lymphoproliferative Syndrome (ALPS); Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS); Immune Reconstitution Inflammatory Syndrome; Wiskott–Aldrich Syndrome.

\item[Infectious \& Postinfectious Syndromes] Toxic Shock Syndrome (TSS); Post-COVID-19 Condition; Post-Polio Syndrome; Congenital Rubella Syndrome; Congenital Zika Syndrome; Waterhouse–Friderichsen Syndrome; Lemierre Syndrome.

\item[Genetic \& Chromosomal Syndromes] Down Syndrome; Turner Syndrome; Klinefelter Syndrome; Edwards Syndrome; Patau Syndrome; DiGeorge Syndrome (22q11.2 Deletion); Fragile X Syndrome; Williams Syndrome; Prader–Willi Syndrome; Angelman Syndrome; Noonan Syndrome; Rett Syndrome; Cri-du-Chat Syndrome; Beckwith–Wiedemann Syndrome; McCune–Albright Syndrome.

\item[Connective Tissue Syndromes] Ehlers–Danlos Syndromes; Marfan Syndrome; Loeys–Dietz Syndrome; Stickler Syndrome; Joint Hypermobility Syndrome.

\item[Pediatric Syndromes] Down Syndrome; Fetal Alcohol Syndrome; Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS); Reye Syndrome; DiGeorge Syndrome; Beckwith–Wiedemann Syndrome; Nephrotic Syndrome; Hand-Foot-and-Mouth Syndrome.

\item[Obstetric Syndromes] HELLP Syndrome; Antiphospholipid Syndrome; Twin-to-Twin Transfusion Syndrome (TTTS); Amniotic Band Syndrome; Fetal Alcohol Syndrome.

\item[Gynecological Syndromes] Polycystic Ovary Syndrome; Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS); Asherman Syndrome; Genitourinary Syndrome of Menopause; Mayer–Rokitansky–Küster–Hauser (MRKH) Syndrome.

\item[Male Reproductive Syndromes] Klinefelter Syndrome; Androgen Insensitivity Syndrome; Kallmann Syndrome; Post-Vasectomy Pain Syndrome.

\item[Cancer-Predisposition \& Paraneoplastic Syndromes] Lynch Syndrome (HNPCC); Li–Fraumeni Syndrome; Von Hippel–Lindau Syndrome; Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia (MEN 1 and 2); Carcinoid Syndrome; Tumor Lysis Syndrome; Superior Vena Cava Syndrome; Paraneoplastic Neurological Syndromes; Cancer Cachexia Syndrome.

\item[Metabolic \& Nutritional Syndromes] Metabolic Syndrome; Refeeding Syndrome; Wernicke–Korsakoff Syndrome; Milk-Alkali Syndrome; Hungry Bone Syndrome; Fanconi–Bickel Syndrome.

\item[Toxicological Syndromes (Toxidromes)] Serotonin Syndrome; Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome; Anticholinergic Syndrome; Cholinergic Syndrome; Sympathomimetic Syndrome; Sedative-Hypnotic Syndrome; Opioid Withdrawal Syndrome; Alcohol Withdrawal Syndrome.

\item[Critical Care Syndromes] Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (SIRS); Multiple Organ Dysfunction Syndrome (MODS); Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS); Abdominal Compartment Syndrome; Fat Embolism Syndrome.

\item[Sleep Syndromes] Obstructive Sleep Apnea Syndrome; Central Sleep Apnea Syndromes; Restless Legs Syndrome; Kleine–Levin Syndrome; Delayed Sleep Phase Syndrome.

\item[Eye \& Cranial Nerve Syndromes] Horner Syndrome; Adie Syndrome; Brown Syndrome; Duane Retraction Syndrome; Charles Bonnet Syndrome; Dry Eye Syndrome; Tolosa–Hunt Syndrome.

\item[ENT \& Otolaryngologic Syndromes] Ramsay Hunt Syndrome; Eagle Syndrome; Empty Nose Syndrome; Gradenigo Syndrome; Samter Triad (Aspirin-Exacerbated Respiratory Disease).

\item[Dermatological Syndromes] Stevens–Johnson Syndrome; Toxic Epidermal Necrolysis; Staphylococcal Scalded Skin Syndrome; Sweet Syndrome; Gorlin Syndrome; Peutz–Jeghers Syndrome; DRESS Syndrome (Drug Reaction with Eosinophilia and Systemic Symptoms).

\item[Vascular Syndromes] Raynaud Syndrome; Superior Vena Cava Syndrome; Thoracic Outlet Syndrome; Subclavian Steal Syndrome; Leriche Syndrome; May–Thurner Syndrome; Nutcracker Syndrome.

\item[Multisystem Syndromes] Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME/CFS); Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome (POTS); Ehlers–Danlos Syndromes; Metabolic Syndrome; Antiphospholipid Syndrome; Behcet Syndrome.
\end{description}
