\noindent\textit{Scope}: This reference-oriented list organizes common human diseases by organ system and clinical domain. It is a curated, high-importance list rather than a prevalence ranking, and some diseases appear under more than one heading where they are clinically relevant to several systems. It is not an exhaustive index of every term in the dictionary. Entries named as syndromes are covered in the companion appendix, Common Syndromes; conditions conventionally classified as disorders are covered in Common Disorders; and clinical findings, lesions, congenital anomalies, and injuries are covered in their own companion appendices. Individual symptoms, signs, and laboratory findings are defined as dictionary entries rather than listed here.
