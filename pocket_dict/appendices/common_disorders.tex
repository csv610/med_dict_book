\small
\noindent\textit{Scope}: This reference-oriented list organizes human conditions conventionally classified as clinical disorders by medical specialty and organ system. In medical nomenclature, disorders represent disruptions to regular bodily structure or function (frequently functional, metabolic, neurobehavioral, or regulatory disturbances), complementing the disease classifications in Common Diseases and clinical signs in Common Syndromes. Every entity listed corresponds to a defined medical concept in the dictionary.
