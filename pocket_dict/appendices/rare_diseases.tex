\small
\noindent\textit{Scope}: This reference-oriented list organizes clinically significant rare diseases (orphan diseases) by organ system and clinical domain. A rare disease is typically defined as one affecting fewer than 1 in 2,000 individuals (EU criteria) or fewer than 200,000 people in the United States. It highlights high-importance benchmark conditions characterized by distinct genetic, metabolic, or autoimmune etiologies.

\medskip
\begin{description}
\item[Genetic \& Chromosomal Diseases] Turner Syndrome; Klinefelter Syndrome; Fragile X Syndrome; Williams Syndrome; Prader–Willi Syndrome; Angelman Syndrome; Noonan Syndrome; 22q11.2 Deletion Syndrome; Cri-du-Chat Syndrome; Hutchinson–Gilford Progeria Syndrome; Cornelia de Lange Syndrome.

\item[Neurological \& Neurogenetic Diseases] Huntington Disease; Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS); Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA); Friedreich Ataxia; Ataxia–Telangiectasia; Rett Syndrome; Batten Disease (Neuronal Ceroid Lipofuscinosis); Creutzfeldt–Jakob Disease; Fatal Familial Insomnia; Progressive Supranuclear Palsy; Multiple System Atrophy; Stiff-Person Syndrome.

\item[Neuromuscular \& Peripheral Nerve Diseases] Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy; Becker Muscular Dystrophy; Facioscapulohumeral Muscular Dystrophy; Limb-Girdle Muscular Dystrophies; Congenital Muscular Dystrophies; Myasthenia Gravis; Charcot–Marie–Tooth Disease; Amyloid Polyneuropathy.

\item[Inborn Errors of Metabolism \& Storage Diseases] Gaucher Disease; Fabry Disease; Pompe Disease; Tay–Sachs Disease; Niemann–Pick Disease; Krabbe Disease; Metachromatic Leukodystrophy; X-Linked Adrenoleukodystrophy; Alkaptonuria; Homocystinuria; Phenylketonuria; Maple Syrup Urine Disease; Von Gierke Disease (GSD I); Mucopolysaccharidoses (Hurler, Hunter, Sanfilippo Syndromes).

\item[Blood \& Bone Marrow Diseases] Hemophilia A; Hemophilia B; Paroxysmal Nocturnal Hemoglobinuria (PNH); Fanconi Anemia; Diamond–Blackfan Anemia; Pure Red Cell Aplasia; Thrombotic Thrombocytopenic Purpura (TTP); Atypical Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome; Cryoglobulinemia; Castleman Disease.

\item[Immune, Autoinflammatory \& Histiocytic Diseases] Severe Combined Immunodeficiency (SCID); Common Variable Immunodeficiency; Chronic Granulomatous Disease; Wiskott–Aldrich Syndrome; Familial Mediterranean Fever; Cryopyrin-Associated Periodic Syndromes (CAPS); Hemophagocytic Lymphohistiocytosis (HLH); Histiocytosis X (Langerhans Cell Histiocytosis).

\item[Rheumatologic \& Autoimmune Diseases] Systemic Sclerosis (Scleroderma); Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis; Microscopic Polyangiitis; Polyarteritis Nodosa; Behçet Disease; Takayasu Arteritis; Eosinophilic Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis (Churg–Strauss); Relapsing Polychondritis; Adult-Onset Still Disease.

\item[Connective Tissue \& Dermatological Genetic Diseases] Ehlers–Danlos Syndromes (Vascular and Classic Types); Marfan Syndrome; Loeys–Dietz Syndrome; Osteogenesis Imperfecta; Pseudoxanthoma Elasticum; Epidermolysis Bullosa; Ichthyosis; Xeroderma Pigmentosum; Pemphigus Vulgaris; Bullous Pemphigoid.

\item[Endocrine Diseases] Addison Disease; Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia; Acromegaly; Cushing Disease (Pituitary-Dependent); Hypoparathyroidism; Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia (MEN 1 and 2); Pheochromocytoma; Autoimmune Polyglandular Syndromes.

\item[Hepatic \& Biliary Diseases] Wilson Disease; Alpha-1 Antitrypsin Deficiency; Primary Biliary Cholangitis; Primary Sclerosing Cholangitis; Biliary Atresia; Progressive Familial Intrahepatic Cholestasis; Caroli Disease.

\item[Renal \& Urinary Diseases] Alport Syndrome; Cystinosis; Primary Hyperoxaluria; Bartter Syndrome; Gitelman Syndrome; Polycystic Kidney Disease (Autosomal Recessive Type); Medullary Cystic Kidney Disease.

\item[Pulmonary Diseases] Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis; Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension; Lymphangioleiomyomatosis (LAM); Pulmonary Alveolar Proteinosis; Primary Ciliary Dyskinesia.

\item[Cardiovascular Diseases] Brugada Syndrome; Long QT Syndrome; Short QT Syndrome; Arrhythmogenic Right Ventricular Cardiomyopathy; Fibromuscular Dysplasia; Takayasu Arteritis.

\item[Gastrointestinal Diseases] Hirschsprung Disease; Achalasia; Eosinophilic Esophagitis; Short Bowel Syndrome; Hereditary Pancreatitis; Microvillus Inclusion Disease.

\item[Eye \& Visual Pathway Diseases] Retinitis Pigmentosa; Stargardt Disease; Leber Hereditary Optic Neuropathy; Leber Congenital Amaurosis; Retinoblastoma; Aniridia; Best Disease.

\item[Bone, Skeletal \& Cartilage Diseases] Achondroplasia; Fibrodysplasia Ossificans Progressiva (FOP); Hypophosphatasia; Osteopetrosis (Marble Bone Disease); Cleidocranial Dysplasia; Thanatophoric Dysplasia.

\item[Rare Infectious \& Prion Diseases] Creutzfeldt–Jakob Disease; Variant CJD; Kuru; Whipple Disease; Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML); Ebola Virus Disease; Marburg Virus Disease; Lassa Fever.

\item[Rare Cancers \& Neoplasms] Malignant Mesothelioma; Gastrointestinal Stromal Tumor (GIST); Neuroendocrine Tumors; Ewing Sarcoma; Osteosarcoma; Retinoblastoma; Uveal Melanoma; Adrenocortical Carcinoma; Thymoma; Merkel Cell Carcinoma; Kaposi Sarcoma; Burkitt Lymphoma.

\item[Multisystem \& Inflammatory Diseases] Erdheim–Chester Disease; POEMS Syndrome; VEXAS Syndrome; IgG4-Related Disease; Systemic Amyloidosis; Castleman Disease.
\end{description}
