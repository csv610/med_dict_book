\noindent\textit{Scope}: A clinical sign is an objective abnormality that can be observed, measured, elicited, or detected by a clinician, unlike a symptom, which is experienced and reported by the patient. For a medical dictionary, the following system-based classification is useful.

\medskip
\begin{description}
\item[General / Vital Signs] Fever; Hypothermia; Tachycardia; Bradycardia; Tachypnea; Bradypnea; Hypertension; Hypotension; Orthostatic Hypotension; Pulsus Paradoxus; Wide Pulse Pressure; Narrow Pulse Pressure; Hypoxemia; Central Cyanosis; Peripheral Cyanosis; Altered Level of Consciousness; Cachexia; Pallor; Jaundice; Dehydration; Pitting Edema; Non-Pitting Edema; Generalized Lymphadenopathy; Clubbing.

\item[Skin Signs] Erythema; Pallor; Cyanosis; Jaundice; Petechiae; Purpura; Ecchymosis; Spider Angiomas; Palmar Erythema; Splinter Hemorrhages; Osler Nodes; Janeway Lesions; Rash; Urticaria; Vesicles; Bullae; Pustules; Papules; Nodules; Ulcers; Scaling; Excoriations; Lichenification; Skin Thickening; Hyperpigmentation; Hypopigmentation; Alopecia; Diaphoresis; Reduced Skin Turgor.

\item[Head \& Face Signs] Facial Asymmetry; Facial Edema; Ptosis; Proptosis; Moon Facies; Mask-Like Facies; Temporal Wasting; Kayser-Fleischer Rings; Arcus Senilis; Xanthelasma; Subconjunctival Hemorrhage.

\item[Eye Signs] Conjunctival Pallor; Conjunctival Injection; Scleral Icterus; Anisocoria; Nystagmus; Strabismus; Ptosis; Proptosis; Papilledema; Absent Pupillary Reflex; Abnormal Red Reflex; Corneal Opacity; Roth Spots; Argyll Robertson Pupil.

\item[Ear, Nose \& Throat Signs] Tympanic Membrane Erythema; Tympanic Membrane Perforation; Otorrhea; Nasal Polyps; Epistaxis; Tonsillar Enlargement; Tonsillar Exudate; Pharyngeal Erythema; Oral Ulcers; Oral Thrush; Strawberry Tongue; Coated Tongue; Koplik Spots; Gingival Hyperplasia; Peritonsillar Bulge; Deviated Uvula.

\item[Neck Signs] Cervical Lymphadenopathy; Goiter; Thyroid Nodule; Neck Stiffness; Elevated Jugular Venous Pressure; Carotid Bruit; Tracheal Deviation; Pemberton Sign.

\item[Cardiovascular Signs] Tachycardia; Bradycardia; Irregular Pulse; Weak Pulse; Bounding Pulse; Pulsus Alternans; Corrigan Pulse; Water-Hammer Pulse; Pulse Deficit; Displaced Apex Beat; Heart Murmur; Systolic Murmur; Diastolic Murmur; Gallop Rhythm; Third Heart Sound; Fourth Heart Sound; Pericardial Friction Rub; Cardiac Thrill; Ventricular Heave; Elevated Jugular Venous Pressure; Peripheral Edema; Delayed Capillary Refill.

\item[Respiratory Signs] Tachypnea; Bradypnea; Apnea; Cyanosis; Accessory-Muscle Use; Nasal Flaring; Chest Retractions; Barrel Chest; Pectus Excavatum; Pectus Carinatum; Asymmetric Chest Expansion; Subcutaneous Emphysema; Reduced Air Entry; Wheezing; Crackles; Rhonchi; Stridor; Bronchial Breath Sounds; Dullness to Percussion; Hyperresonance; Pleural Friction Rub.

\item[Abdominal \& Gastrointestinal Signs] Abdominal Distension; Abdominal Tenderness; Guarding; Rigidity; Rebound Tenderness; Shifting Dullness; Fluid Wave; Caput Medusae; Palpable Abdominal Mass; Hepatomegaly; Splenomegaly; Ascites; Succussion Splash; Abnormal Bowel Sounds; Abdominal Bruit; Visible Peristalsis; Rectal Bleeding; Melena.

\item[Neurological Signs] Altered Consciousness; Disorientation; Abnormal Gait; Festinating Gait; Spastic Hemiplegic Gait; Ataxia; Tremor; Asterixis; Fasciculations; Muscle Wasting; Pronator Drift; Cogwheel Rigidity; Lead-Pipe Rigidity; Abnormal Muscle Tone; Hyperreflexia; Hyporeflexia; Areflexia; Pathological Reflexes; Clonus; Dysmetria; Dysdiadochokinesia; Sensory Deficit; Motor Weakness on Examination; Cranial Nerve Deficit; Nystagmus; Neck Rigidity.

\item[Musculoskeletal Signs] Joint Swelling; Joint Deformity; Joint Effusion; Restricted Range of Motion; Crepitus; Muscle Wasting; Muscle Spasm; Heberden Nodes; Bouchard Nodes; Swan-Neck Deformity; Boutonniere Deformity; Baker Cyst; Limb Deformity; Abnormal Posture; Scoliosis; Kyphosis; Lordosis; Abnormal Gait.

\item[Renal \& Urinary Signs] Bladder Distension; Costovertebral-Angle Tenderness; Peripheral Edema; Hematuria on Testing; Proteinuria; Oliguria; Anuria.

\item[Endocrine Signs] Goiter; Exophthalmos; Tremor; Abnormal Hair Distribution; Hirsutism; Striae; Acanthosis Nigricans; Moon Facies; Buffalo Hump; Peripheral Edema.

\item[Hematological Signs] Pallor; Petechiae; Purpura; Ecchymoses; Generalized Lymphadenopathy; Splenomegaly; Hepatomegaly; Active Bleeding.

\item[Gynecological Signs] Abnormal Vaginal Bleeding on Examination; Vaginal or Cervical Discharge; Cervical Lesions; Pelvic Mass; Uterine Enlargement; Adnexal Mass; Cervical-Motion Tenderness; Chandelier Sign.

\item[Male Genital Signs] Testicular Swelling; Testicular Mass; Scrotal Edema; Penile Discharge; Genital Ulceration; Phimosis; Hydrocele; Varicocele.

\item[Pediatric \& Neonatal Signs] Poor Weight Gain on Growth Charts; Growth Retardation; Bulging Fontanelle; Sunken Fontanelle; Neonatal Jaundice; Central Cyanosis; Chest Retractions; Abnormal Primitive Reflexes; Gowers Sign.

\item[Named Clinical Signs] Babinski Sign; Murphy Sign; Kernig Sign; Brudzinski Sign; Rovsing Sign; Cullen Sign; Grey Turner Sign; Trousseau Sign; Chvostek Sign; Romberg Sign; Hoffmann Sign; Courvoisier Sign; Kussmaul Sign; Battle Sign; Nikolsky Sign; Auspitz Sign; Ortolani Sign; Barlow Sign; Trendelenburg Sign; McBurney Sign; Tinel Sign; Phalen Sign; Lhermitte Sign; Blumberg Sign; Kehr Sign; Hamman Sign; Homans Sign; Psoas Sign; Obturator Sign; Lachman Sign; Anterior Drawer Sign.
\end{description}

\medskip
\noindent\textit{Clinical Note}: A comprehensive medical reference can contain 500--1,000+ clinical signs, encompassing physical examination findings, named signs, dermatological signs, radiological signs, and laboratory markers.
