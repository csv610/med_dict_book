% Common injuries, poisoning, and environmental health conditions.
\small
\noindent\textit{Scope}: This companion list covers injuries (trauma), poisoning and envenomation, and adverse effects of environmental exposure and extremes. These are events or conditions rather than diseases; it complements the appendices of Common Diseases, Common Disorders, and Common Syndromes.

\medskip
\begin{description}
\item[Bone, Joint \& Muscle Injuries] Fracture; Dislocation; Sprain; Strain.

\item[Skin \& Soft-Tissue Injuries] Contusion; Laceration; Abrasion; Crush Injury.

\item[Nervous-System Injuries] Traumatic Brain Injury; Concussion; Spinal-Cord Injury.

\item[Thermal \& Electrical Injuries] Burns; Electrical Injury; Hypothermia; Frostbite; Heat Exhaustion; Heatstroke.

\item[Environmental Conditions] Drowning; Dehydration; Altitude Sickness; Motion Sickness.

\item[Poisoning \& Envenomation] Carbon Monoxide Poisoning; Lead Poisoning; Mercury Poisoning; Arsenic Poisoning; Pesticide Poisoning; Snakebite; Scorpion Sting.

\item[Drug \& Alcohol Overdose] Drug Overdose; Alcohol Poisoning.
\end{description}
