\noindent\textit{Scope}: For a medical dictionary or disease-reference project, symptoms are best organized by body system, because a flat alphabetical list quickly becomes difficult to use. Here is a broad list of common medical symptoms.

\medskip
\noindent\textit{Clinical Note}: For a comprehensive medical dictionary, it is essential to distinguish symptoms (subjective experiences reported by the patient, such as nausea or pain) from clinical signs (objectively observed physical findings detected on examination, such as jaundice, edema, or tachycardia). Mixing them is common in general health lists, but an authoritative reference maintains clear separation among Symptoms, Clinical Signs, Syndromes, and Diseases/Disorders categories.
