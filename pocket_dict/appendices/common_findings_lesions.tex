% Common clinical findings, lesions, symptom patterns, and obstetric events.
\small
\noindent\textit{Scope}: This companion list collects clinical findings, structural lesions, symptom patterns, and obstetric events that are not diseases in themselves but are frequently encountered in clinical work, imaging, and laboratory testing. It complements the appendices of Common Diseases, Common Disorders, and Common Syndromes.

\medskip
\begin{description}
\item[Cardiovascular \& Respiratory Findings] Pericardial Effusion; Pleural Effusion; Pneumothorax; Hemothorax; Atelectasis.

\item[Abdominal \& Pancreatic Findings] Esophageal Varices; Gallstones; Biliary Obstruction; Cholestasis; Pancreatic Pseudocyst; Pancreatic Cysts; Pancreatic Necrosis.

\item[Renal \& Urinary Findings] Kidney Stones; Hydronephrosis.

\item[Head, Neck \& Airway Findings] Thyroid Nodules; Nasal Polyps; Deviated Nasal Septum; Vocal-Cord Nodules; Earwax Impaction; Tympanic Membrane Perforation.

\item[Oral \& Dental Findings] Tooth Erosion; Impacted Teeth; Malocclusion; Geographic Tongue.

\item[Eye \& Skin Findings] Chalazion; Stye; Keloids; Ingrown Toenail.

\item[Genital \& Reproductive Findings] Ovarian Cysts; Bartholin Cyst; Varicocele; Hydrocele; Spermatocele.

\item[Pain \& Symptom Conditions] Low-Back Pain; Sciatica.

\item[Metabolic Risk \& Allergic States] Prediabetes; Anaphylaxis.

\item[Obstetric \& Labor Events] Ectopic Pregnancy; Miscarriage; Recurrent Pregnancy Loss; Placenta Previa; Placental Abruption; Placenta Accreta Spectrum; Premature Rupture of Membranes; Preterm Labor; Fetal Growth Restriction; Obstructed Labor; Postpartum Hemorrhage.
\end{description}
