\small
\noindent\textit{Classification}: The first groups show the most common inheritance pattern. The later clinical categories overlap, and several conditions have rare alternate patterns or genetically heterogeneous forms.

\medskip
\begin{description}
\item[Autosomal Dominant] Achondroplasia; Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease (ADPKD); Familial Adenomatous Polyposis; Familial Hypercholesterolemia; Hereditary Angioedema (most forms); Huntington Disease; Marfan Syndrome; Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia; Neurofibromatosis Type 1; Neurofibromatosis Type 2; Tuberous Sclerosis Complex; Von Hippel--Lindau Disease.

\item[Autosomal Recessive] Alpha-Thalassemia; Beta-Mannosidosis; Beta-Thalassemia; Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia; Cystic Fibrosis; Familial Mediterranean Fever; Gaucher Disease; Hemochromatosis; Phenylketonuria (PKU); Sickle-Cell Disease; Spinal Muscular Atrophy; Tay--Sachs Disease; Wilson Disease.

\item[X-Linked] Becker Muscular Dystrophy; Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy; Fragile X Syndrome; Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase (G6PD) Deficiency; Hemophilia A; Hemophilia B; X-Linked Agammaglobulinemia.

\item[Variable or Genetically Heterogeneous] Alport Syndrome (X-linked, autosomal dominant, or autosomal recessive); Charcot--Marie--Tooth Disease (multiple inheritance patterns); Hereditary Spherocytosis (usually autosomal dominant, sometimes autosomal recessive); Retinitis Pigmentosa (autosomal dominant, autosomal recessive, X-linked, or mitochondrial); Von Willebrand Disease (usually autosomal dominant, with some autosomal recessive forms).

\item[Mitochondrial] MELAS; MERRF; Leber Hereditary Optic Neuropathy (LHON).

\item[Inherited Metabolic Disorders] Maple Syrup Urine Disease; Galactosemia; Homocystinuria; Glycogen Storage Diseases; Lysosomal Storage Diseases; Urea-Cycle Disorders.

\item[Inherited Neurological or Neuromuscular Disorders] Charcot--Marie--Tooth Disease; Spinal Muscular Atrophy; Hereditary Spastic Paraplegia; Friedreich Ataxia.

\item[Inherited Blood Disorders] Sickle-Cell Disease; Alpha-Thalassemia; Beta-Thalassemia; Hereditary Spherocytosis; Hemophilia A; Hemophilia B; some forms of Von Willebrand Disease.

\item[Inherited Connective-Tissue or Skeletal Disorders] Osteogenesis Imperfecta; Ehlers--Danlos Syndromes; Marfan Syndrome; Achondroplasia.

\item[Inherited Renal Disorders] Alport Syndrome; Autosomal Dominant Polycystic Kidney Disease (ADPKD); Autosomal Recessive Polycystic Kidney Disease (ARPKD); Cystinuria.

\item[Inherited Eye Disorders] Retinitis Pigmentosa; Stargardt Disease; congenital color-vision deficiencies; some forms of congenital cataract.

\item[Hereditary Cancer Syndromes] Lynch Syndrome; Familial Adenomatous Polyposis; Hereditary Breast and Ovarian Cancer Syndrome; Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia (MEN) Syndromes; Li--Fraumeni Syndrome; Von Hippel--Lindau Disease.
\end{description}
