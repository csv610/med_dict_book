\textbf{Important:} This is a documentation form, not a treatment protocol. Stabilize and manage urgently according to current local/regional guidance, the specific antivenom product information, and poison-centre or specialist advice. Antivenom selection, indications, dose, and first-aid recommendations vary by region and snake species.
