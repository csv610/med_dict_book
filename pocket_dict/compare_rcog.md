# RCOG A–Z medical terms checked against the dictionary

Source: [Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists — A–Z of medical terms](https://www.rcog.org.uk/for-the-public/a-z-of-medical-terms/).
Review date: 2026-09-08.

The RCOG A–Z is the source list for this audit. It contains 390 glossary entries in the reviewed page: **305** are represented in the dictionary and **85** are absent as equivalent terms.

## Matching policy

- Each item below comes from the RCOG A–Z page, not from the dictionary.
- Dictionary membership was checked against headwords and synonym lines extracted directly from `chapters/med_terms_*.tex`.
- Comparison ignores capitalization, punctuation, repeated whitespace, and standard UK/US spelling variants.
- When an RCOG entry includes an abbreviation, the expanded full term must be present as the dictionary headword or synonym; an abbreviation alone does not count as a match.
- A related term is not treated as an exact match; absent explanations therefore identify the missing RCOG wording rather than unrelated dictionary entries.
