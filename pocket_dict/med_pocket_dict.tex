\section*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Preface}

\noindent This dictionary is a concise reference to medical terms, diseases, investigations, treatments, anatomy, physiology, and related clinical concepts. It is intended to help students, healthcare professionals, educators, and general readers understand unfamiliar medical language.

\medskip

\noindent The entries are arranged alphabetically from A to Z, with each letter presented in its own chapter. This arrangement allows a reader to locate a known term quickly while maintaining a consistent structure throughout the book. Related terms, recognized common names, and alternative names are included when they improve clarity or aid searching.

\medskip

\noindent Definitions are written in simple, formal language and are intended to be clear, accurate, and useful for reference. Medical terminology changes as scientific knowledge and clinical guidance develop; therefore, important information should be checked against current textbooks, primary research, professional guidance, and local clinical protocols.

\medskip

\noindent This book is an educational reference, not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Readers should seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional when making decisions about health.

\medskip

\noindent\textit{Chaman Singh Verma}
