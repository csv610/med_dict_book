## Critical Issues Found

### 1. Cross-Chapter Duplicates (~150 terms)
**Examples with full entries in multiple chapters:**
- `Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia` — Hematology + Oncology (identical text)
- `Antiphospholipid Syndrome` — Hematology + Rheumatology (Rheumatology more detailed)
- `Hodgkin Lymphoma` — Hematology + Oncology (Hematology more detailed)
- `Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia` — Hematology + Oncology
- `Diabetes Mellitus` — Endocrinology + multiple others

**Policy needed:** Canonical chapter per term + cross-references (`\medterm{Term}-- See Canonical Chapter.`)

### 2. Chapter Size Disparity
- Largest: Obstetrics & Gynecology (436), Pharmacology (310), Pulmonology (301)
- Smallest: General (4), Medical Implants (38), Biochemistry (44)
- **Target:** Minimum 50 entries per chapter for credible coverage

### 3. LaTeX Compilation Warnings
- 10+ underfull hbox warnings (hyphenation issues in two-column mode)
- No errors — build is stable
- Consider `\sloppy` or adjusted `\hyphenpenalty` for two-column

### 4. Outdated/Verification Needed
- **Oncology:** Verify TNM 8th edition staging throughout
- **Cardiology:** ACC/AHA 2023 HF guidelines, 2024 AFib updates
- **Pediatrics:** Vaccine schedule (COVID, RSV, MenB updates)
- **Dermatology:** JAK inhibitor FDA labels (ruxolitinib cream, upadacitinib)
- **Rheumatology:** ACR/EULAR 2021 RA, 2019 SLE criteria current
