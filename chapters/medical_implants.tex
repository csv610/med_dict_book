\chapter{Medical Implants}

\medterm{Artificial Ventricle (Total Artificial Heart)}-- A pulsatile, pneumatically or electrically driven biventricular replacement that removes the native ventricles. Indications: end-stage heart failure bridging to transplant or destination therapy. Requirements: surgical implantation, external power source, anticoagulation. Complications: thromboembolism, infection, hemolysis, right heart failure.

\medterm{Biliary Stent}-- Tube placed in bile duct to relieve obstruction. Types: plastic (short-term, cheaper, higher occlusion rate), self-expanding metal (longer-term, patent longer). Indications: malignant obstruction (pancreatic cancer, cholangiocarcinoma), benign stricture. Complications: migration, occlusion, cholangitis, pancreatitis.

\medterm{Bone Cement (Polymethylmethacrylate—PMMA)}-- Acrylic polymer used to fix prosthetic joints to bone. Mixes as dough, cures exothermically. Used in hip/knee arthroplasty, vertebroplasty, cranial reconstruction. Complications: cement implantation syndrome (hypotension, hypoxia), infection, loosening.

\medterm{Cochlear Implant}-- Electronic device bypassing damaged hair cells to stimulate auditory nerve directly. Components: external processor, transmitter, electrode array. Indications: severe-to-profound sensorineural hearing loss. Critical period for implantation: before age 5 for optimal speech development.

\medterm{Coronary Stent}-- Mesh tube keeping coronary artery open after angioplasty. Types: bare-metal (higher restenosis), drug-eluting (releases antiproliferative drug, lower restenosis but prolonged DAPT). Complications: stent thrombosis, restenosis, bleeding (from DAPT).

\medterm{Cranial Implant}-- See neurosurgical implants.

\medterm{Defibrillator Implant}-- See pacemaker/ICD.

\medterm{Drain (Surgical)}-- Tube for fluid evacuation. Types: closed suction (Jackson-Pratt, Hemovac), open (Penrose), pigtail. Indications: abscess, hematoma, seroma, biliary, urinary. Complications: infection, blockage, migration.

\medterm{Endovascular Graft}-- Stent-graft for aortic aneurysm repair (EVAR/TEVAR). Fabric tube on metal stent frame. Delivered via femoral arteries. Indications: AAA >5.5 cm, symptomatic aneurysm, descending thoracic aneurysm. Surveillance: CT angiography annually.

\medterm{Foreign Body}-- Any object lodged in body. Types: organic (wood, thorn), inorganic (metal, glass, plastic). Radiopaque vs. radiolucent. Removal: surgical, endoscopic, spontaneous passage. Complications: infection, granuloma, migration.

\medterm{Gastrostomy Tube (PEG)}-- Tube through abdominal wall into stomach for feeding. Percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy. Indications: prolonged enteral feeding, dysphagia, neurological disorders. Complications: infection, leakage, granulation tissue, burial bumper.

\medterm{Heart Valve Prosthesis}-- Replacement heart valve. Types: mechanical (titanium, carbon, durable, requires lifelong anticoagulation), bioprosthetic (cow/pig tissue, no anticoagulation, degenerates). Mechanical: more durable, higher thrombotic risk. Bioprosthetic: better quality of life, reoperation risk.

\medterm{Hip Prosthesis}-- Total hip replacement components: femoral stem (metal), head (metal/ceramic), acetabular cup (polyethylene/metal/ceramic). Fixation: cemented (older patients), cementless (younger, porous coating for bone ingrowth). Materials: cobalt-chrome, titanium, ceramics. Complications: dislocation, infection, loosening, wear debris, periprosthetic fracture.

\medterm{Intraocular Lens (IOL)}-- Artificial lens replacing natural lens after cataract extraction. Types: monofocal (distance vision), multifocal/bifocal (near and distance), toric (astigmatism), extended depth of focus. Implantation via small incision, folds for insertion. Complications: posterior capsule opacification, dysphotopsia, infection, retinal detachment.

\medterm{Joint Prosthesis}-- See arthroplasty.

\medterm{Laryngectomy Tube}-- Tube maintaining airway after total laryngectomy. Types: silicone, metal. Indications: laryngeal cancer, trauma. Complications: obstruction, infection, granulation.

\medterm{Lithotripsy Probe}-- See vascular surgery.

\medterm{Mammary Implant}-- Breast augmentation/reconstruction device. Types: saline (silicone shell, saline fill), silicone gel (cohesive gel). Incisions: inframammary, periareolar, axillary. Placement: subglandular or submuscular. Complications: capsular contracture, rupture, infection, BIA-ALCL (anaplastic large cell lymphoma).

\medterm{Nasal Stent}-- Support for nasal septum/airway. Materials: silicone, metal. Indications: septal hematoma, post-septoplasty support, nasal valve collapse. Complications: crusting, infection, pressure necrosis.

\medterm{Ostomy Appliance}-- Collection device for feces/urine after ostomy surgery. Types: urostomy (urine), colostomy (feces), ileostomy (ileum). One-piece or two-piece systems. Open or closed end. Skin care essential. Complications: skin irritation, hernia, obstruction, prolapse.

\medterm{Orthopedic Implant}-- Devices for bone/joint reconstruction. Types: plates, screws, rods, hinges, prostheses. Materials: titanium, cobalt-chrome, stainless steel, polyethylene, ceramic. Used for fractures, arthritis, tumors, deformities. Complications: infection, loosening, fracture, wear.

\medterm{Pacemaker}-- Implantable device for cardiac arrhythmia management. Components: pulse generator (battery, circuit), leads (electrodes). Types: single-chamber, dual-chamber, biventricular (CRT). Indications: bradyarrhythmia, heart failure (CRT), syncope. Complications: infection, lead displacement, pneumothorax, battery depletion.

\medterm{Penile Prosthesis}-- Inflatable or malleative device for erectile dysfunction. Types: two-piece, three-piece (reservoir, pump, cylinders). Surgical implantation. Complications: infection, erosion, mechanical failure, dissatisfaction.

\medterm{Prosthetic Limb}-- Artificial replacement for missing limb. Types: above/below knee, above/below elbow. Components: socket, suspension, joint, pylon, terminal device. Materials: carbon fiber, silicone, metal. Rehabilitation: gait training, strength, ADL.

\medterm{Stent}-- Tubular scaffold maintaining vessel/duct patency. Materials: metal (stainless steel, cobalt-chrome, nitinol), polymer. Types: bare-metal, drug-eluting. Applications: coronary, peripheral, biliary, ureteral, tracheal. Complications: restenosis, thrombosis, migration, fracture.

\medterm{Tracheostomy Tube}-- See medical instruments.

\medterm{Ureteral Stent}-- Double-J stent maintaining ureteral patency. Indications: obstruction (stone, stricture, malignancy), post-ureteroscopy. Materials: silicone, polyurethane. Sizes: 4.7-7 Fr, lengths 24-30 cm. Complications: irritation, hematuria, encrustation, migration.

\medterm{Ventricular Assist Device (VAD)}-- Mechanical pump supporting heart function. Types: left (LVAD), bi-ventricular (BiVAD), right (RVAD). Indications: end-stage heart failure, bridge to transplant/cardiomyectomy. Complications: bleeding, infection, stroke, pump thrombosis. Continuous-flow devices now standard.

\medterm{Shunt}-- Tube diverting fluid. Types: ventriculoperitoneal (CSF), ventriculoatrial, lumboperitoneal, ventriculopleural. Indications: hydrocephalus. Complications: obstruction, infection, overdrainage, migration. Valves regulate pressure.

\medterm{Aneurysm Clip}-- Surgical clip occluding aneurysm neck. Materials: titanium, stainless steel. Indications: intracranial aneurysm. Placed via craniotomy. Complications: clip migration, vessel occlusion, rebleeding.

\medterm{Cardiac Resynchronization Therapy (CRT)}-- Biventricular pacing for heart failure with dyssynchrony. Paces both ventricles simultaneously. Indications: LBBB, QRS >150 ms, EF <35%. Improves symptoms, reduces hospitalizations, mortality.

\medterm{Drug-Eluting Stent}-- Mesh tube keeping coronary artery open after angioplasty. See coronary stent.

\medterm{Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR) Implant}-- Not standard; may refer to biomaterials research.

\medterm{Foley Catheter}-- See medical instruments.

\medterm{Glucose Sensor}-- Continuous glucose monitor (CGM) for diabetes. Subcutaneous sensor measures interstitial glucose. Transmitter sends data to receiver/phone. Examples: Dexcom, Freestyle Libre, Medtronic. Reduces hypoglycemia, improves A1c.

\medterm{Hearing Aid}-- Amplification device for hearing loss. Types: behind-the-ear (BTE), in-the-ear (ITE), receiver-in-canal (RIC). Components: microphone, amplifier, speaker, battery. Fitting: audiogram-based. Adjustments: volume, programs.

\medterm{Intra-aortic Balloon Pump (IABP)}-- Mechanical circulatory support. Inflatable catheter in descending aorta. Inflates during diastole (increases coronary perfusion), deflates during systole (reduces afterload). Indications: cardiogenic shock, unstable angina, high-risk PCI. Complications: limb ischemia, bleeding, infection.

\medterm{Joint Replacement}-- See arthroplasty.

\medterm{Knee Prosthesis}-- Total knee replacement. Components: femoral component (metal), tibial component (metal + polyethylene insert), patellar button (optional). Fixation: cemented or cementless. Indications: end-stage osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, post-traumatic. Outcomes: pain relief, function improvement, longevity 15-20+ years.

\medterm{Lithotripter Probe}-- See medical instruments.

\medterm{Magnetoresistive Sensor}-- See radiology.

\medterm{Needle}-- Hollow metal tube for fluid withdrawal or injection. See medical instruments.

\medterm{Orthopedic Screws}-- Fixation devices for fractures. Materials: titanium, stainless steel. Types: cortical, cancellous, interfragmentary. Used with plates, rods, nails. Complications: loosening, breakage, infection.

\medterm{Penile Implant}-- Inflatable or malleative device for erectile dysfunction. See penile prosthesis.

\medterm{Retinal Prosthesis}-- Experimental device for blindness. Argus II system: camera to processor to electrode array stimulates retina. Indications: retinitis pigmentosa. Provides light perception, pattern recognition.

\medterm{Sacral Nerve Stimulation}-- Neuromodulation for urinary/fecal incontinence, constipation. Sacral nerve root (S3) stimulation. Implantable pulse generator + electrode. Indications: refractory urge incontinence, fecal incontinence, non-obstructive urinary retention.

\medterm{Tissue Expander}-- Balloon implanted under skin/muscle, gradually filled with saline to expand tissue. Used for breast reconstruction, scalp reconstruction, burn coverage. Removed after expansion, replaced with permanent implant or flap.

\medterm{Urethral Sling}-- Synthetic or fascial tape supporting urethra for stress incontinence. Mid-urethral sling (TVT, TOT) standard. Minimally invasive. Complications: erosion, urgency, retention.

\medterm{Ventriculoperitoneal Shunt}-- Tube diverting fluid. See shunt.

\medterm{Wound VAC}-- Vacuum-assisted closure for wound healing. Foam dressing connected to vacuum pump. Removes exudate, reduces edema, promotes granulation. Used for complex wounds, surgical sites, pressure ulcers.

\medterm{Brachytherapy Seed}-- Radioactive source implanted directly into tumor. I-125 or Pd-103 seeds for prostate cancer. Permanent or temporary implantation.

\medterm{Collagen Implant}-- Biocompatible scaffold for tissue engineering. Types: bovine, porcine, synthetic. Used for hernia repair, soft tissue augmentation.

\medterm{Dental Implant}-- Titanium fixture placed in jawbone for tooth replacement. Osseointegration: bone fusion around implant. Success rate: >95% at 10 years.

\medterm{Esophageal Stent}-- Self-expanding metal stent for esophageal obstruction. Palliative for malignancy. Migration, tumor ingrowth common complications.

\medterm{Fascial Loop}-- Autologous or synthetic material for surgical reinforcement. Midurethral sling uses synthetic tape.

\medterm{Grid Implant}-- See mesh implant.

\medterm{Hernia Mesh}-- Synthetic or biologic material for hernia repair. Polypropylene most common. Complications: infection, recurrence, chronic pain, mesh migration.

\medterm{Intravascular Filter}-- Device placed in inferior vena cava to prevent pulmonary embolism. See IVC filter.

\medterm{IVC Filter}-- Device placed in inferior vena cava to prevent pulmonary embolism. Indications: DVT/PE with contraindication to anticoagulation, recurrent PE despite anticoagulation. Complications: filter thrombosis, migration, perforation, IVC occlusion.

\medterm{Joint Replacement}-- See arthroplasty.

\medterm{Knotless Anchor}-- Suture anchor for soft tissue repair without tying knots. Used in orthopedic surgery.

\medterm{Laser Fiber}-- Optical fiber for laser delivery in surgery. Used in lithotripsy, tumor ablation.

\medterm{Magnetic Seed}-- Sentinel lymph node localization using magnetic seed. Alternative to radioactive tracer.

\medterm{Nasal Implant}-- Support for nasal septum/airway. See nasal stent.

\medterm{Ostomy Appliance}-- Collection device for feces/urine after ostomy surgery. See ostomy appliance.

\medterm{Patch Graft}-- Vascular graft for patch angioplasty. Bovine pericardium, PTFE.

\medterm{Retinal Prosthesis}-- Experimental device for blindness. See retinal prosthesis.

\medterm{Sacral Nerve Stimulator}-- Neuromodulation for urinary/fecal incontinence, constipation. See sacral nerve stimulation.

\medterm{Stent Graft}-- Stent-graft for aortic aneurysm repair (EVAR/TEVAR). See endovascular graft.

\medterm{Tissue Expander}-- Balloon implanted under skin/muscle, gradually filled with saline to expand tissue. See tissue expander.

\medterm{Urethral Sling}-- Synthetic or fascial tape supporting urethra for stress incontinence. See urethral sling.

\medterm{Ventricular Assist Device}-- A mechanical pump that supports heart function in patients with advanced heart failure. See ventricular assist device.

\medterm{Wound VAC}-- Vacuum-assisted closure for wound healing. See wound VAC.

\medterm{Aneurysm Coils}-- Platinum coils for aneurysm embolization. Detachable coils, balanced coil formation.

\medterm{Balloon Catheter}-- Inflatable balloon for angioplasty, stent deployment. Non-compliant balloons for high-pressure dilation.

\medterm{Catheter}-- Flexible tube for drainage, infusion, or device delivery. Materials: silicone, polyurethane, PVC. Sizes: French (Fr) scale.

\medterm{Depot Implant}-- Long-acting drug delivery implant. Hormonal contraceptives, antipsychotics. Subdermal placement.

\medterm{Endoprosthesis}-- Internal prosthetic device. Vascular stent-grafts, airway stents.

\medterm{Foreign Body}-- Any object lodged in body. See foreign body.

\medterm{Graft}-- Transplanted tissue or synthetic material. Autograft (self), allograft (donor), xenograft (animal), synthetic.

\medterm{Heart Valve}-- Replacement heart valve. See heart valve prosthesis.

\medterm{Implantable Cardioverter Defibrillator (ICD)}-- An implanted electronic device that delivers electrical impulses to the heart to regulate heart rate and rhythm. See pacemaker.

\medterm{Joint Prosthesis}-- See joint prosthesis.

\medterm{Keratoprosthesis}-- Artificial cornea for severe corneal blindness. Boston KPro most common. Indications: chemical burns, limbal stem cell deficiency.

\medterm{Laryngeal Prosthesis}-- Voice prosthesis after laryngectomy. Tracheoesophageal puncture, one-way valve.

\medterm{Mesh}-- Synthetic or biologic material for hernia repair. See hernia mesh.

\medterm{Needle}-- Hollow metal tube for fluid withdrawal or injection. See medical instruments.

\medterm{Ostomy}-- Surgical opening into bowel or urinary tract. Colostomy, ileostomy, urostomy. Appliances for collection.

\medterm{Prosthesis}-- Artificial replacement for missing body part. Limb, eye, breast, dental.

\medterm{Screws}-- Fixation devices for fractures. See orthopedic screws.

\medterm{Stent}-- Tubular scaffold maintaining vessel/duct patency. See stent.

\medterm{Tissue Expander}-- Balloon implanted under skin/muscle, gradually filled with saline to expand tissue. See tissue expander.

\medterm{Urethral Sling}-- Synthetic or fascial tape supporting urethra for stress incontinence. See urethral sling.

\medterm{Vascular Graft}-- Synthetic or biologic conduit for vascular reconstruction. PTFE, Dacron, saphenous vein.

\medterm{Wound VAC}-- Vacuum-assisted closure for wound healing. See wound VAC.

\medterm{X-ray Shield}-- Lead apron, thyroid shield for radiation protection during imaging.

\medterm{Yoke}-- Not applicable in implants.

\medterm{Z-Plasty}-- Surgical technique for contracture release using Z-shaped incision. Lengthens tissue, changes scar orientation.

\medterm{Abrasion}-- Superficial skin injury. Management: cleaning, topical antibiotics, dressing.

\medterm{Biocompatibility}-- Ability of material to function without adverse response. ISO 10993 standards for biocompatibility testing.

\medterm{Catheter}-- Flexible tube for drainage, infusion, or device delivery. See catheter.

\medterm{Debridement}-- Removal of dead tissue from wound. Sharp, autolytic, enzymatic, mechanical methods.

\medterm{Enthesis}-- Attachment site of tendon/ligament to bone. Implant fixation often targets enthesis.

\medterm{Fibrin Sealant}-- Biological adhesive for surgical closure. Thrombin + fibrinogen. Hemostasis, sealant.

\medterm{Graft}-- Transplanted tissue or synthetic material. See graft.

\medterm{Host Response}-- Immunological reaction to implant. Foreign body response, encapsulation, rejection.

\medterm{Implantable Loop Recorder}-- Subcutaneous device providing continuous ECG monitoring for up to 3 years. See emergency medicine.

\medterm{Juxta-articular}-- Near joint. Periprosthetic fracture, periprosthetic infection.

\medterm{Keratinocyte}-- Primary cell type in epidermis. Used in wound healing research, skin grafts.

\medterm{Ligament}-- Fibrous connective tissue connecting bones. ACL, PCL, MCL, LCL knee ligaments. Reconstruction with graft.

\medterm{Mesh}-- See mesh implant.

\medterm{Necrosis}-- Death of tissue. Pressure necrosis from implant, ischemic necrosis.

\medterm{Osteointegration}-- Direct structural connection between living bone and implant surface. Titanium implants achieve this.

\medterm{Prosthesis}-- An artificial device such as a hearing aid, artificial joint, or dentures that substitutes for a missing body part. See prosthesis.

\medterm{Remodeling}-- Bone adaptation to implant stress. Wolff's law: bone remodels along lines of stress.

\medterm{Scaffold}-- Three-dimensional structure for tissue engineering. Porous, biodegradable. Cells populate scaffold.

\medterm{Tendon}-- Fibrous connective tissue connecting muscle to bone. Rotator cuff, Achilles, biceps tendon. Repair with suture, anchor.

\medterm{Ulnar}-- Not applicable in implants. See anatomy.

\medterm{Vascularity}-- Blood supply to tissue. Implants require vascularization for integration. Angiogenesis essential.

\medterm{Wound Healing}-- Complex process of tissue repair. Phases: hemostasis, inflammation, proliferation, remodeling. Implant integration depends on healing.

\medterm{Xenograft}-- Transplanted tissue or synthetic material. See graft.

\medterm{Yield Strength}-- Stress at which material begins to deform plastically. Important for implant design.

\medterm{Zirconia}-- Zirconium dioxide ceramic. Biocompatible, strong. Used in dental implants, hip prostheses.
