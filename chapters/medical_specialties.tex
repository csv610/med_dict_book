\chapter{Medical Specialties}

\noindent This appendix provides a comprehensive reference of medical specialties, their primary focus, and the organ systems or patient populations they serve. Specialties are organized by their primary classification: medical (internal medicine), surgical, diagnostic, and multidisciplinary.
