\medterm{Surgical Instruments}-- Instruments for surgery. Categories: cutting (scalpel, scissors), grasping (forceps, clamps), retraction, hemostasis (hemostat, electrocautery).

\medterm{Suture (Perineal)}-- A thread or strand of material used to approximate tissues (bring wound edges together) and hold them in place until healing occurs. See Suture.

\medterm{Syringe}-- Cylindrical device for injecting or withdrawing fluids. Components: barrel, plunger, luer lock. Various sizes: 1 mL to 60 mL.

\medterm{Thermometer}-- Device measuring body temperature. Types: mercury (phased out), digital, tympanic, temporal artery, infrared. Normal oral: 36.5-37.5°C.

\medterm{Thrombectomy (Vascular)}-- See Thrombectomy Device.

\medterm{Ventilator}-- Mechanical device providing ventilatory support. Modes: assist-control, SIMV, CPAP, PRVC. Parameters: tidal volume, respiratory rate, FiO2, PEEP.
